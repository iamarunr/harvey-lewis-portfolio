## Documentary

### *Like Harvey Like Son* (2018/2020)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Subject | Harvey Lewis III's 2018 Appalachian Trail FKT attempt with his father (Harvey Lewis Jr., 78) as crew chief |
| Start date | May 30, 2018 |
| Route | Springer Mountain, GA → Mount Katahdin, ME (2,190 miles) |
| Time | 49 days 14 hours |
| Director | Rudy Harris Jr. |
| Producers | TJ Bitter, Mike Trimpe, Rudy Harris Jr. |
| Editor | Lindsey Michels |
| Executive Producer | Edward Wimmer IV |
| Co-Executive Producers | Erin Eimer, Rudy Harris Jr., Tammi Harris |
| Trailer | https://vimeo.com/383788991 |
| Website | https://www.likeharveylikeson.com/ |
| Streaming | Amazon Prime Video |
| Award | **Best Feature Film** — Woodbury Film Festival, Salt Lake City, UT (2019) |
| Official Selection | Northeast Mountain Film Festival (Dillard, GA) |
| Official Selection | Hunter Mountain Film Festival (Hunter, NY) |
| Cincinnati premiere | Nov 10 & 12, 2020 — Mariemont Theatre, 7 p.m. |
| Press coverage | WVXU radio interview — Harvey Lewis + director Rudy Harris Jr. |
| Notes | Title has double meaning: both Harvey Lewis III and his father are named "Harvey Lewis." Father was estranged early in Harvey's youth; film is partly a reconciliation story. |
