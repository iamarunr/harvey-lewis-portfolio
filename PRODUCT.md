# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Sponsors and brand managers evaluating a partnership; race directors and media booking interviews or appearances; documentary viewers and running fans arriving from podcasts (Rich Roll) or race coverage (iRunFar). They arrive already curious; the site must convert curiosity into respect within one screen.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio site for Harvey Lewis: two-time Badwater 135 champion, two-time backyard ultra world record holder (450 miles / 108 hours), and full-time high school teacher in Cincinnati. The site is his professional front door: proof of achievement (124 races since 1996), the human story (teacher, vegan, run-commuter), and a contact surface for speaking, sponsorship, and media. Success: a sponsor or journalist leaves with the two facts that define him (450-mile world record; he teaches school the same week) and a way to reach him.

## Brand Personality

Gritty, editorial, understated. The register of a long-form magazine profile, not a sports-drink ad. Confidence expressed through restraint and evidence: real numbers, real quotes, real photographs. He is the antithesis of flash; the site must be too.

## Anti-references

- Glossy SaaS/landing-page aesthetics: gradients (banned outright by the owner), glassmorphism, rounded-everything, purple-blue palettes.
- Generic "AI-made" template look: hero-metric clichés, identical card grids, stock-photo energy.
- Loud sports-brand maximalism (Nike campaign pages, Strava clones): oversized parallax, video autoplay, hype copy ("BEAST MODE").
- Visual noise: overlapping text on busy photo regions, competing typographic styles in one viewport.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over hype.** Numbers, dates, and sourced quotes carry the persuasion. Never adjectives.
2. **The duality is the story.** Teacher by day, record-holder by night; every page should let the ordinary amplify the extraordinary.
3. **Photography carries emotion; type carries information.** Never make text fight a photograph for attention.
4. **Print discipline.** Hairline rules, mono microlabels, sharp corners, flat color. If it wouldn't work on paper, question it.
5. **One voice per viewport.** Each screenful has a single dominant element; everything else recedes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Dark surfaces require ≥4.5:1 body-text contrast (sand #EDE4D3 on charcoal passes). Respect prefers-reduced-motion for any scroll or hover animation. Semantic headings, alt text on all photography.
