## Key Story Angles for the Website

1. **The Teacher Who Runs to Work** — Every school day, he runs 3 miles from East Walnut Hills to SCPA, backpack loaded, sometimes racing the bus when his students are aboard. Started ~2013 after a promise to his students following a climate change film.

2. **The Broken Neck** — ~2004: surgeons rebuilt his C4-C5 vertebrae with titanium. He became faster after. "I try to encourage people not to put mental limitations on their abilities."

3. **The Vegan Origin** — His mother (a nurse) had a stroke at 54. He was 19. That same year he went vegetarian and ran his first ultra.

4. **Two Harvey Lewises** — His full name is Harvey Lewis III. His father (Harvey Lewis Jr., then 78) crewed his 2018 Appalachian Trail run. The documentary *Like Harvey Like Son* is about both — and the reunion of estranged father and son across 2,190 miles.

5. **The Honey Badger** — He picks a spirit animal for every race. For Big's Backyard 2021 (85 laps, 354 miles, first world record), he was the Honey Badger.

6. **48 Hours Later, He Went to Teach** — After finishing 450 miles in 108 hours at Big's 2023, Harvey slept in a tent, drove home to Cincinnati, and was back in his classroom on Friday morning. "He had to go teach." *(iRunFar)*

7. **The Gandhi Runner** — In 2008, he earned a grant to retrace Gandhi's 241-mile Salt March through India. In 2009, he ran MLK's Selma to Montgomery march and met 99-year-old civil rights icon Amelia Boynton Robinson.

8. **World's Toughest Footrace — Twice** — Two Badwater 135 wins (2014, 2021), the second at age 45 after coming from 1 hour behind at mile 90.
