## Badwater 135

The marquee event of his career. Harvey Lewis has started Badwater 15+ times, making him one of the most prolific competitors in the race's history.

### 2014 — First Win
- **Date**: July 21–23, 2014 (AM start)
- **Place**: 1st overall
- **Time**: ~23:53 (Wikipedia: "just under 23 hours and 53 minutes" — exact time unverified from race database)
- **Notes**: First major victory. He was a teacher at SCPA in Cincinnati at the time. Cincinnati Enquirer headline: "SCPA teacher finishes 1st in 135-mile ultramarathon" (Jack Walsworth, July 24, 2014). His profile in PodiumRunner shortly after: "The Crazy Running Life Of Harvey Lewis."

### 2021 — Second Win
- **Date**: July 19, 2021
- **Place**: 1st overall (1st men's)
- **Time**: 25:50:23
- **Age at win**: 45
- **Narrative**: Spent most of the race in a three-man battle with Igor Gotsuliak (Ukraine) and Sergey Ionov (Russia). Was nearly one hour behind Gotsuliak at mile 90. Passed him and opened a one-hour lead by mile 122. His 10th Badwater finish, 5th podium, 2nd win. (Source: iRunFar, Sam Anderson, July 21, 2021)
- **Other men's finishers**: 2nd Gotsuliak 26:35:08, 3rd Ionov 26:49:06

## Big Dog's Backyard Ultra

Appearances at the world's most prestigious backyard ultra event in Bell Buckle, Tennessee. Two wins, both world records at the time, and pushing past 110 yards.

### Format
4.166667-mile loop ("yard") starting every hour. Runners alternate between a trail loop (day) and a road loop (night). Last person standing wins; they must then complete a solo yard to be official.

### 2017 — The Assist (Runner-Up)
- **Laps**: 58 (241.67 miles)
- **Place**: 2nd overall / "the assist"
- In backyard ultra, there is no official second place — the runner-up is called the "assist."

### 2018 — Early Exit
- **Laps**: 4
- **Place**: 66th overall
- Details unknown — did not advance far.

### 2021 — First Win (World Record)
- **Date**: October 16–19, 2021
- **Laps**: 85 (354.16 miles in ~85 hours)
- **Place**: 1st (last person standing) — new world record at the time
- **Previous world record**: 68 laps
- **Final three**: Harvey Lewis (Cincinnati, OH), Terumichi Morishita (Toyota City, Japan), Chris Roberts (Kirkwood, MO)
- **Narrative**: Lewis entered as a favorite, having twice previously gotten "the assist." The final three raced into a third day. Morishita dropped at 85 laps; Roberts stepped off. Lewis completed his solo 86th yard to claim the win with a fractured hand.
- (Source: iRunFar, Alex Potter, October 19, 2021)

### 2023 — Second Win (New World Record)
- **Date**: October 21–25, 2023
- **Laps**: 108 (450 miles in 108 hours)
- **Place**: 1st (last person standing) — new backyard world record
- **Previous world record**: 102 yards (Phil Gore, Dead Cow Gully Backyard Ultra, Australia, June 2023)
- **Six runners surpassed 100 hours** — unprecedented
- **The 100-hour barrier**: Merijn Geerts (Belgium), Phil Gore (Australia), Terumichi Morishita (Japan) all fell between yards 100–102
- **Final three**: Harvey Lewis, Ihor Verys (Ukraine/Canada), Bartosz Fudali (Poland)
- They broke Gore's record at lap 103. Lewis was last standing at 108.
- (Source: iRunFar, Sarah Brady, October 25, 2023)

### 2024
- **Distance**: 316.66 miles (76 laps)
- **Place**: 4th overall (3rd gender)

### 2025
- **Distance**: 462.51 miles (111 laps)
- **Place**: 3rd overall
- Exceeded his own 2023 world-record distance (450 miles), surviving 111 consecutive hours on foot.

## Appalachian Trail — 2018 FKT Attempt

- **Year**: 2018
- **Distance**: ~2,190 miles (Springer Mountain, GA to Mount Katahdin, ME)
- **Result**: Completed — did **not** set FKT
- **Time**: 49 days, 14 hours
- **What makes this remarkable**: He finished the AT and then, just **five days later**, started the 2018 Badwater 135 (finished 38th, 38:55:30).
- Documentary: *Like Harvey Like Son* (Harris Media Co.) — chronicles this attempt
- (Source: iRunFar "This Week in Running," July 23, 2018: "The man missed the Appalachian Trail FKT but still finished in a remarkable 49 days, 14 hours. That finish was unbelievably only five days before the start of Badwater.")

## Chasing Democracy 250 — 2026

- **Year**: 2026 (Late June – Early July)
- **Distance**: 250 miles
- **Route**: Liberty State Park (Jersey City, NJ / Statue of Liberty) to Washington, D.C. (finishing at the Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial)
- **Duration**: 5 days
- **Significance**: Commemoration of America's 250th anniversary (Semiquincentennial, America250). Designed to look beyond the history books and honor the diverse, often marginalized communities that forged American democracy.
- **Role**: Runner 01 and lead media contact.
- **Team**: Harvey Lewis, Greg Armstrong, Gregg Lawson, Kristen Soinski, with support crew Carolina Rubio-MacWright and Naresh Kumar.
- **Website**: [chasingdemocracy250.com](https://chasingdemocracy250.com/) | Live tracker: [mlk-run.pages.dev](https://mlk-run.pages.dev/)
- **Charity Beneficiaries**: *Touching Land* and *Rising Hearts*.
