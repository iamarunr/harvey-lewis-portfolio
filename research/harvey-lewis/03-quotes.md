## 2. Big Dog's Backyard Ultra

> "There was a time where it was unfathomable that someone could make it four days on Big Dog's Backyard. Of course, even the idea of finishing a third day was once thought impossible. Here there were 22 runners that made it to 300 miles. It's a beautiful thing."

— [iRunFar, "A Different Mode," Nov 10, 2023](https://www.irunfar.com/harvey-lewis-2023-big-dogs-backyard-ultra-interview)

---

> "I think that it's possible I could have kept on going all the way through that next night. I was moving very well, I didn't have any injuries, I was continuously fueled by good vegan foods."

— [iRunFar, "A Different Mode," Nov 10, 2023](https://www.irunfar.com/harvey-lewis-2023-big-dogs-backyard-ultra-interview)

---

> "I just feel my ribs are a little bruised, but other than that I feel really great and mentally I'm really excited about the possibilities that Big's opened up in terms of pushing even further limits."

— [iRunFar, "A Different Mode," Nov 10, 2023](https://www.irunfar.com/harvey-lewis-2023-big-dogs-backyard-ultra-interview) *(post-race, within hours of finishing 450 miles)*

---

> "The night before the race this year, I had one of the three worst nights of sleep I've ever had before a race. I could only fall asleep for three hours, I was too wound up mentally."

— [iRunFar, "A Different Mode," Nov 10, 2023](https://www.irunfar.com/harvey-lewis-2023-big-dogs-backyard-ultra-interview)

---

> "Honestly in this race this year, I was more tired in day one and day two than I was in 2021."

— [iRunFar, "A Different Mode," Nov 10, 2023](https://www.irunfar.com/harvey-lewis-2023-big-dogs-backyard-ultra-interview)

---

> "Well, since I won Big's, I can kind of get away with saying that whoever wins gets an automatic entry into the Barkley Marathons. That would definitely have to be the thing that's going to keep me up some nights."

— [iRunFar, "A Different Mode," Nov 10, 2023](https://www.irunfar.com/harvey-lewis-2023-big-dogs-backyard-ultra-interview)

---

> "About 170 miles in, I was in a dark spot, and he's like, just imagine you're going out for a run to work every time you do that nighttime loop. That was golden — I could keep doing that forever."

— [Trail Running Magazine, Oct 2021](https://runningmagazine.ca/trail-running/harvey-lewis-ran-final-few-yards-at-bigs-with-a-hand-fracture/) *(on advice from crewman Judd Poindexter during Big's 2021)*

## 3. Sleep Strategy & Recovery

> "He suggested that I take two deep breaths and then exhale and do that 10 times to calm the nervous system."

— [iRunFar, "A Different Mode," Nov 10, 2023](https://www.irunfar.com/harvey-lewis-2023-big-dogs-backyard-ultra-interview) *(on advice from Todd Anderson, Dream Recovery)*

---

> "It wasn't until the fourth night that I really got to the mode where I could fall asleep for a minute or two, because I was running 55-minute loops, so it wasn't a lot of time to sleep."

— [iRunFar, "A Different Mode," Nov 10, 2023](https://www.irunfar.com/harvey-lewis-2023-big-dogs-backyard-ultra-interview)

---

> "Recovery time and the ability for the body to rebalance is a massive benefit."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

> "I did a 24 hour race in September and did a 100 mile trail race just 13 days later. I won the 24 race and came second at the 100 mile race. That was only five weeks before the Big Dog Ultra. Just having that quick recovery!"

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

> "Last year I got the course record on the 146 mile route at Badwater and two weeks later I ran the biggest 100 mile race at Ohio and I won that race. It shows you how quickly the body can recover."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

## 4. Plant-Based Diet & Nutrition

> "I was continuously fueled by good vegan foods."

— [iRunFar, "A Different Mode," Nov 10, 2023](https://www.irunfar.com/harvey-lewis-2023-big-dogs-backyard-ultra-interview)

---

> "Race fuel doesn't make me sick. I eat something big and don't get sick. Other people are eating meat and dairy and it just doesn't process. You can process more calories at a quicker pace with plant foods, and that translates to more energy over time."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

> "I've been vegetarian for 25 years and I've been vegan for five years."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/) *(said days after 2021 Big Dog's win)*

---

> "It's the single most important ingredient to my longevity in the sport."

— [No Meat Athlete / various sources](https://www.nomeatathlete.com/podcast/radio-37/) *(on veganism)*

---

> "We would do a lot better if we move toward more plant-based foods for the benefit of curbing the No. 1 killer (diseases) in America."

— [CityBeat, Nov 2020](https://www.citybeat.com/cincinnati/a-look-inside-the-life-of-cincinnati-ultramarathon-runner-and-educator-harvey-lewis/Slideshow/12233155)

---

> "The possibilities that lie out there in 10, 20, 30 years for dealing with things like climate change, health, aging population — this is huge. And it's a cheap fix. This is a very inexpensive way of making a dramatic difference."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

> "I love squeezable chia seed fruit, miso soup, foods with sodium and fats. I like variety. I also drink coconut water and aloe juice. Beans and rice is really nice. Cooked with spices it's perfect."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

> "I love different ethnic foods. Bean burritos, stir fry, quinoa and vegetables, soups — as long as it's vegan, I'm happy to have it."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

## 5. Becoming Vegan — The Origin Story

> "Back in high school I ate what was in my culture. My mom was a busy nurse, she didn't have a lot of time so we ate a lot of fast food. Meals were heavier on meat. I was unhealthy overall as a kid, I could grab layers of fat around my stomach, and I always had irregularity using the rest room. I had heartburn, challenges like that."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

> "I loved animals, my cat, my dog, my fish. But I didn't think anything and would just grab something from McDonald's. Something was picking at me."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

> "When I was 19 my mother had a stroke. She was 54 years old, a nurse, and we had no family history. The nutrition was a big element that we could have done differently."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

> "I went full vegan through the remainder of college in 1998."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)

---

> "I also had some exposure early on in the ultra community from vegetarians. I think Yiannis Kouros was vegan at that time."

— [Great Vegan Athletes](https://www.greatveganathletes.com/harvey-lewis-ultramarathon-runner/)
