Who he is

Harvey Lewis is a high school teacher in Cincinnati who has twice set the backyard ultra world record.

Two-time Badwater 135 champion, twice a backyard ultra world record setter, five-time Team USA 24-hour athlete, 124 races since 1996. He run-commutes three miles each way to school and calls every student a sequoia.
